Uneven feed shows up in your flock before it shows up in your books.

When one batch differs from the next, growth rates slip, birds fall behind on weight and mortality creeps up. You pay for it in extra days on the shed and lost margin.

Our answer is precision blending. Each formulation is weighed, mixed and tested to the same specification, so the bag you open in March gives your birds the same nutrition as the one you open in June.
